How to run it

1. Click Open TV window and drag that window onto the TV (or cast that tab). Click it once so it goes fullscreen with sound.
2. Players pick a category and value. Click that square. The question shows on the TV, the answer only shows here.
3. Someone says "ding", you rule on it, add or take points, then hit Reveal.
4. Suggested chaos: right answer = spin the Good Wheel. Every few turns, give the dragon a turn. Rig anything you want. Nobody can tell.
